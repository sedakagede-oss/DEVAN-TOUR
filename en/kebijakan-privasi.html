Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

This policy explains what data is collected when you use the DEVAN TOUR website on Cloudflare Pages.

Data we collect

  • Booking form: name, country/origin (optional), WhatsApp number or email, tour package, duration, tour date, number of guests, pickup location, and notes.
  • Review form: name, star rating (1–5), and comment text.
  • Anti-spam: IP address only to rate-limit review submissions (short TTL in Cloudflare KV), not for marketing.

How data is used & stored

Booking data is sent to our WhatsApp so we can confirm your tour. Review data is stored in Cloudflare KV; new reviews stay pending and appear only after we approve them. We do not sell your personal data.

Hosting & third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Social links go to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Deposit payment is arranged on WhatsApp (QRIS / e-wallet); we do not store card numbers on the website.

Your rights

To request deletion of booking or review data, email gededevan725@gmail.com or WhatsApp +62 858-6963-3160.
