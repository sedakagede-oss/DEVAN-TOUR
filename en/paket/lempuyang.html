Lempuyang Full-Day Tour — Gates of Heaven & Tirta Gangga

⏱ Full day (8–10 hours)

About This Tour

A day exploring quieter East Bali: the split gate of Lempuyang Temple with Mount Agung beyond — known as the Gates of Heaven — then the water palace of Tirta Gangga, Taman Ujung on the coast, the white sand of Virgin Beach, and Sang Hyang Ambu Temple in the Bugbug hills with views toward the sea.

Lempuyang Tour Itinerary

  1. 1

    Lempuyang Temple (Gates of Heaven)

    Pura Penataran Agung Lempuyang, on the slopes of Mount Lempuyang in Karangasem. Its split gate frames Mount Agung when the sky is clear, and is widely known as the Gates of Heaven. Wear modest clothing plus a sarong (kamen) and sash in the temple area.

  2. 2

    Tirta Gangga

    The former royal water palace of Karangasem: pools, a tiered fountain, stepping stones and lush gardens. A gentle place to walk and photograph the water and stone carvings.

  3. 3

    Taman Ujung (Ujung Water Palace)

    Taman Sukasada Ujung, a water palace on the Karangasem coast, with a red-roofed pavilion on the pools, bridges, and green views toward the hills.

  4. 4

    Virgin Beach (Pasir Putih, Karangasem)

    Pasir Putih Beach (Pantai Perasi) in Karangasem: a light-sand cove on Bali’s east coast, with clear water and a calmer feel than the busy southern beaches. A good place to swim and unwind.

  5. 5

    Sang Hyang Ambu Temple

    A carved gate on Sang Hyang Ambu Hill in Bugbug, Karangasem. From here you can look out to the sea and the surrounding hills — a quiet close to a day in East Bali. Keep belongings secure if wild monkeys are around.

You may request destinations, provided they are on this list: Lempuyang Temple (Gates of Heaven), Tirta Gangga, Taman Ujung (Ujung Water Palace), Virgin Beach (Pasir Putih, Karangasem), and Sang Hyang Ambu Temple. The order may change depending on traffic and weather.
