Tur Uluwatu & Kecak

⏱ Full day (8–10 hours) / Half day

About This Tour

A day exploring southern Bali: water sport at Tanjung Benoa, the iconic Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK) statue, kopi luwak, paragliding, then Kecak dance and Pura Luhur Uluwatu.

Uluwatu Tour Itinerary

Tujuan boleh request, dengan syarat destinasi yang diminta ada di daftar berikut: water sport Tanjung Benoa, GWK, kopi luwak, paragliding, serta Kecak & Uluwatu Temple.

  1. 1

    Water Sports — Tanjung Benoa

    Tanjung Benoa in the Nusa Dua area is one of the most popular beaches in southern Bali for water sports. Its waters tend to be calm, ideal for banana boat rides, jet skiing, parasailing and other water activities. Pick what suits your nerve — from relaxed family fun to adrenaline-pumping thrills!

  2. 2

    GWK — Garuda Wisnu Kencana

    A cultural park in Ungasan featuring the giant statue of Lord Vishnu riding Garuda, created by Balinese sculptor I Nyoman Nuarta. At around 121 metres including its base, it is one of the tallest statues in the world. Explore the grand limestone-cliff plazas and take photos with this iconic statue as your backdrop.

  3. 3

    Kopi Luwak — Coffee Plantation

    Stop at a coffee plantation to see how coffee beans are processed the traditional way, from roasting to grinding. Taste kopi luwak — the famous Indonesian coffee made from beans that have passed through a civet — along with other Balinese coffees and teas. A great place to pick up coffee souvenirs.

  4. 4

    Paragliding — Southern Coastal Cliffs

    Feel the thrill of a tandem flight with an experienced pilot above the coastal cliffs of southern Bali. As you glide on the sea breeze, enjoy views of the coastline, the Indian Ocean surf and the limestone cliffs from above. Flights depend on wind and weather conditions.

  5. 5

    Kecak & Pura Luhur Uluwatu

    Pura Luhur Uluwatu stands at the edge of a sheer cliff about 70 metres above the Indian Ocean. Around sunset, watch the Kecak dance on the open-air stage near the temple: dozens of male dancers chant “cak-cak-cak” as they tell the story of the Ramayana. Wear modest clothing plus a sarong (kamen) and sash in the temple area, and keep your belongings safe from the monkeys.

The order may change depending on traffic and weather.
