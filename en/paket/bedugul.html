Bedugul Full-Day Tour — Ulun Danu, Handara & Bloom Garden

⏱ Full day (8–10 hours)

About This Tour

A full day in Bali's cool, green Bedugul highlands: wander the flower beds of The Bloom Garden, see Pura Ulun Danu Beratan seeming to float on Lake Beratan, photograph the iconic Handara Gate, stop at Bali Farm House, then take in the lake views from Wanagiri Hidden Hill.

Bedugul Tour Itinerary

  1. 1

    The Bloom Garden

    A flower garden in the Candikuning highlands, close to Lake Beratan. Wander colourful beds, walking paths and photo spots in the cool mountain air of Bedugul.

  2. 2

    Ulun Danu Beratan Temple

    A sacred water temple on the shore of Lake Beratan, its tiered meru shrines often seeming to float on the water. One of Bali’s most recognisable views, set between quiet hills and the lake.

  3. 3

    Handara Gate

    An iconic split gate at the entrance to Handara Golf Resort. The road runs between two stone towers with green mountains beyond — one of Bedugul’s signature photo spots.

  4. 4

    Bali Farm House

    A stop in a highland farm setting: gardens, farm animals, and a place to rest for lunch or coffee in the cool air of the Wanagiri area.

  5. 5

    Wanagiri Hidden Hill

    A viewpoint on the Wanagiri ridge overlooking Lake Buyan and Lake Tamblingan. Enjoy the twin-lake panorama, forest air and photo spots above the hills.

You may request destinations, provided they are on this list: The Bloom Garden, Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, Handara Gate, Bali Farm House, and Wanagiri Hidden Hill. The order may change depending on traffic and weather.
