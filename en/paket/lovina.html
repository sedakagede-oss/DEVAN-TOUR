Lovina Full-Day Tour — Dolphins, Hot Springs & Aling-Aling

⏱ Full day (8–10 hours)

About This Tour

A full day escaping to Bali's quiet, less-touristy north coast: sunrise dolphins in Lovina, black-sand beaches, Brahmavihara Arama temple, Banjar hot springs, Krisna watersport & souvenirs, then Aling-Aling Waterfall in the jungle — with a scenic drive over the mountains that is part of the adventure itself.

Lovina Tour Itinerary

  1. 1

    Lovina Dolphin Tour

    Set out early onto Lovina's calm sea. From a traditional jukung outrigger, watch wild dolphins surfacing at sunrise — the signature moment of Bali's north coast.

  2. 2

    Brahmavihara Arama Temple

    Bali's largest Buddhist monastery, tucked into the Banjar hills. Wander quiet courtyards, stupas and a split gateway with green views — a peaceful pause after the morning at sea.

  3. 3

    Banjar Hot Springs

    Natural hot springs set in a tropical garden. Soak in open pools under stone dragon spouts — a refreshing reset before the next stop.

  4. 4

    Krisna Watersport & Souvenirs

    Stop for watersport and souvenirs: try a banana boat or other water games (optional), then browse Balinese keepsakes before heading to the waterfall.

  5. 5

    Aling-Aling Waterfall

    A jungle waterfall in North Bali. Descend to a natural pool framed by cliffs and tropical green — perfect for photos, a quick swim and cool mountain air.

The order may change depending on traffic and weather.
