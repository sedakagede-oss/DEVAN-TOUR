Ubud Tour Full Day — Rafting, ATV & Tirta Empul

⏱ Full day (8–10 hours)

About This Tour

Satu hari penuh petualangan dan budaya di sekitar Ubud: memacu adrenalin di arus sungai dan jalur ATV, berayun di atas hutan kelapa dan sawah Alas Harum, bertemu burung dan reptil di Bali Bird & Reptile Park, lalu menutup hari dengan suasana sakral Tirta Empul Temple.

Ubud Tour Itinerary

Tujuan boleh request, dengan syarat destinasi yang diminta ada di daftar berikut: Rafting, ATV, Alas Harum, Bali Bird & Reptile Park, dan Tirta Empul Temple.

  1. 1

    Rafting — Ayung River

    White-water rafting on the Ayung, Bali's longest river, which cuts through green valleys around Ubud. With a raft, helmet and experienced guides, you ride exciting rapids while taking in mossy cliffs, tropical forest and small waterfalls along the way.

  2. 2

    ATV — Jalur Desa & Sawah

    Ride an ATV (quad bike) along dirt tracks, muddy puddles, rice fields and village plantations. Beginner-friendly — you get a briefing and safety gear before setting off. Expect to come back muddy and grinning!

  3. 3

    Alas Harum — Ayunan & Agrowisata

    Alas Harum in Tegallalang is an agro-tourism spot overlooking rice terraces and coconut forest. Try its famous high swings for a photo floating above the valley, explore unique photo spots, and taste coffee and tea grown in Bali.

  4. 4

    Bali Bird & Reptile Park

    A wildlife park in the Singapadu area of Gianyar with colourful tropical birds — such as cockatoos, macaws and the rare Bali starling — plus a reptile collection. A fun and educational stop, great for families and children.

  5. 5

    Tirta Empul Temple

    A holy water temple in Tampaksiring built around a natural spring. Balinese Hindus come here for melukat — a purification ritual under the sacred water spouts. Visitors may take part when dressed respectfully in a sarong (kamen) and sash.

The order may change depending on traffic and weather.
