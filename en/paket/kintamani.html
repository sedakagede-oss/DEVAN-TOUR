Kintamani Tour Full Day — Penglipuran, Akasa & Tirta Empul

⏱ Full day (8–10 hours)

About This Tour

A day exploring the highlands and cultural villages on the Kintamani route: tidy traditional Penglipuran Village, lunch or coffee with a Mount Batur view at Akasa resto, the Sari Timbul art garden, the cool Ulu Petanu waterfall, then ending at Tirta Empul Temple.

Kintamani Tour Itinerary

  1. 1

    Penglipuran Village

    A traditional village in Bangli known as one of the tidiest in Bali. Clean stone lanes lined with thatched traditional houses, with no motor vehicles inside the village. A calm place to see everyday Balinese life and photograph the iconic bamboo pathways.

  2. 2

    Akasa resto

    A cafe and restaurant on the Penelokan cliff in Kintamani with a direct view of Mount and Lake Batur. Ideal for a lunch stop or Kintamani Arabica coffee overlooking the caldera. The cool highland air makes this break especially refreshing.

  3. 3

    Sari Timbul

    An art and craft garden in the Sebatu area of Tegallalang — known for its fairytale-garden atmosphere, glass works and unique photo spots overlooking a green valley. Visitors can watch glassblowing, try the swings and explore art installations set in nature.

  4. 4

    Ulu Petanu waterfall

    A waterfall in the Kedisan area of Tegallalang, hidden in tropical forest with mossy cliffs and a clear pool. Access is relatively easy via stairs; great for photos, a quick dip and cool air before continuing to the next stop.

  5. 5

    Tirta Empul Temple

    A holy water temple in Tampaksiring built around a natural spring. Balinese Hindus come here for melukat — a purification ritual under the sacred water spouts. Visitors may take part when dressed respectfully in a sarong (kamen) and sash.

The order may change depending on traffic and weather.
